Activation

Modifier plus key, e.g. Ctrl+Shift+Space. Hold to talk; with "Confirm before inserting" on, press once to start and once to end instead. A shortcut already taken by another application is rejected and the previous one stays active.

Discards a transcript waiting for confirmation, or cancels a take your engine is still processing, in either activation mode. Must differ from the dictation shortcut.
